## 🌟 Overview

**A-One** is a modern, fully interactive online bookstore and stationery shop featuring a unique **magical sparkle animation** that flies books into your shopping cart. Built as a single-page application with zero dependencies, it delivers a premium shopping experience optimized for both desktop and mobile devices.

> 💡 **Fun Fact:** Click any book cover or the "+" button to watch it fly into your cart with a trail of sparkles!

---

## ✨ Features

### 🛍️ Shopping Experience
- **Magical Fly-to-Cart Animation** — Watch books soar into your cart with sparkle trails
- **Slide-Out Cart Drawer** — Smooth, interactive shopping bag with live totals
- **Real-Time Calculations** — Subtotal, tax, and shipping update instantly
- **Free Shipping Progress Bar** — Gamified progress toward free shipping

### 💳 Multi-Payment Checkout
- 💳 **Credit/Debit Card** — Secure card payment form
- ⚡ **Cryptocurrency** — USDT, BTC, ETH with QR code & wallet addresses
- 📦 **Cash on Delivery** — Traditional doorstep payment option

### 📱 Responsive Design
- **Mobile-First** — Optimized for low-bandwidth mobile networks
- **Touch-Friendly** — 44px+ tap targets with `touch-action: manipulation`
- **Accessible (WCAG)** — Full keyboard navigation, ARIA labels, focus rings
- **Modern Images** — Auto-served AVIF/WebP via CDN for 50% smaller file sizes

### 🎨 Premium UI/UX
- Custom **A-One SVG Logo** — Stylized "A" as an open book with magic sparkle
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** typography for a modern, friendly feel
- **Brand Colors** — Purple (#3b1c6e) & Coral (#f68e7a) theme
- **Smooth Animations** — Cart bounce, badge pop, sparkle decay effects
